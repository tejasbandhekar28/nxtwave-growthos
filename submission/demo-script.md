# 3-Minute Demonstration Script: NxtWave GrowthOS

**Target Audience:** Evaluators, Growth Leads, and Engineering Stakeholders  
**Total Running Time:** 3 Minutes (180 Seconds)  

---

### Minute 0:00 – 0:45: Context, Neumorphic Aesthetic & Campaign Goal
- **Spoken:**
  > *"Welcome to NxtWave GrowthOS. We built this application to execute and monitor a high-velocity 7-day growth campaign targeting 500 final-year engineering students for NxtWave's free hands-on workshop: 'Build Your First AI Project in 60 Minutes'.*
  > 
  > *Notice the visual language right away: we have implemented a pure Neumorphic Soft UI design system inspired by modern audio consoles and soft tactile physical interfaces. The main canvas uses pale sky blue (`#DDF5FF`), light blue raised surfaces (`#D9F0FA`), and dual-directional soft white and blue box shadows, paired with high-contrast navy typography for crisp readability."*
- **Action on Screen:**
  - Pan across the Overview Dashboard.
  - Hover over the KPI cards to show subtle elevation microinteractions.
  - Highlight the Campaign Milestone card showing **342 of 500 confirmed students (68%)**.

---

### Minute 0:45 – 1:30: Real-Time Growth Analytics & Unit Economics
- **Spoken:**
  > *"On the dashboard, GrowthOS tracks every key metric deterministically. We can see our 7-day daily trend curve comparing actual confirmed velocity against our required target pace. Right now, on Day 5, we need approximately 79 registrations per day over the remaining two days to cross our 500 goal.*
  > 
  > *Looking at our Acquisition Channels donut chart, College Clubs lead the pack at 37% of registrations, followed closely by our viral Student Referral program at 33.3%.*
  > 
  > *If we look at our Budget Accounting panel, out of the total ₹2,000 budget, we've only spent ₹650 so far, achieving a blended acquisition cost of just ₹1.90 per student while keeping ₹1,000 in performance reserve."*
- **Action on Screen:**
  - Point to the Area Chart (Recorded vs. Planned trajectory).
  - Hover over the Acquisition Channels Donut chart.
  - Scroll down to the College Distribution horizontal bar chart showing VNR VJIET and CBIT at the top.

---

### Minute 1:30 – 2:15: Student Registration & Instant Referral Engine
- **Spoken:**
  > *"Now let's switch to the student experience by navigating to the Student Registration page. Here, final-year engineering students see a clean, distraction-free neumorphic card.*
  > 
  > *Let's register an engineering student. Notice the inset input fields, branch selection dropdown, and graduation year validation. When I click 'Register for Free', the application securely submits the record, verifies duplicate prevention on the normalized email, triggers celebratory feedback, and immediately generates a unique, personalized referral code.*
  > 
  > *With one click, the student can copy their referral link or share directly to their college WhatsApp group. This simple mechanism is what powers our viral K-factor of 2.71."*
- **Action on Screen:**
  - Click 'Registration Page' in the sidebar.
  - Type a student name and email.
  - Submit the form to show the celebration animation and the generated student referral code.
  - Click 'Copy Referral Link'.

---

### Minute 2:15 – 3:00: Referral Leaderboard, Fraud Protection & Architecture
- **Spoken:**
  > *"Navigating to the Referral Program screen, GrowthOS displays our verified campus champions. Notice that leaderboard rankings are computed exclusively from confirmed student registrations—preventing fake self-referrals or unverified email padding.*
  > 
  > *Finally, on the System Settings tab, you can see our dual-architecture setup: the frontend connects natively to Supabase PostgreSQL for ACID storage and Row Level Security, and communicates with our local n8n workflow for automated daily briefings and webhook events. If external credentials are not set, GrowthOS operates seamlessly in transparent Demo Mode.*
  > 
  > *In summary, NxtWave GrowthOS combines a premium neumorphic design with rigorous growth accounting to turn student enthusiasm into measurable workshop attendance. Thank you!"*
- **Action on Screen:**
  - Click 'Referral Program' tab to show the leaderboard.
  - Click 'Settings & Config' to demonstrate the Supabase and n8n environment indicators.
  - Return to the Overview page.
